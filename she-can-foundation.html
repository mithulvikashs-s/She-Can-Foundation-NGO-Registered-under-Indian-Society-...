About Us

Global Vision,
Local Action

We are She Can Foundation, a non-governmental organization registered under the Indian Society Act, 1860, dedicated to empowering women and creating a more equitable society.

We provide support, resources, and training to women in communities across the globe, working closely with local organizations, governments, and communities to ensure that our programs are effective and sustainable.

Through advocacy campaigns and initiatives, we raise awareness of women's issues. We rely on the support of individuals, corporations, and other organizations to achieve our vision. We believe that by working together, we can revolutionize society and create a better world for all.

Our Story

Welcome to
She Can Foundation

NGO Registered Under the Indian Society Act, 1860

She Can Foundation was born from a simple yet powerful belief — that every woman deserves the chance to rise. Since our founding, we have been on the ground in communities across India and beyond, turning that belief into action.

What started as a small volunteer effort has grown into a structured organization with real programs, real impact, and real lives changed — one woman at a time.
